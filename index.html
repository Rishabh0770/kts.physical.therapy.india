About Dr. Kushal K. Tiwari

Kushal Kumar Tiwari, also known as Physio KT, was born on 16 September 1987 in Khandwa, Madhya Pradesh. He completed his BPT at Himalayan University and his MPT in Sports Rehabilitation from IEC University, Himachal Pradesh. A former Indian Scouts volunteer with defence and rescue training, he is also a Black Belt Taekwondo athlete who won gold in the 2017 World Team Poomsae Championship in Bangkok.

Dr. Tiwari has served as a team physiotherapist for the All India Football Federation, Indian Olympic Association sports teams, Hockey Chandigarh, Minerva Academy FC, Delhi FC, and RoundGlass Sports. He is known for introducing professional sports-specific hydrotherapy rehabilitation in India and supporting national-level athletes through injury recovery and high performance training.
