At KT's Physical Therapy India, we deliver premium physiotherapy and rehabilitation services focused on sports performance, injury recovery, and human movement excellence. We specialize in advanced sports rehabilitation, musculoskeletal physiotherapy, injury prevention, and performance enhancement for athletes and active individuals.

Our mission is to help every patient recover efficiently, restore confidence in movement, and achieve peak physical performance through personalized, evidence-based care.

Combining clinical expertise with modern rehabilitation techniques, we provide a comprehensive approach that focuses not only on pain relief but also on long-term strength, mobility, and functional recovery. Every treatment program is designed with precision, professionalism, and a commitment to excellence.

At KT's Physical Therapy Facility, we believe recovery is not just about healing injuries — it is about rebuilding stronger, moving better, and performing at your best.

Why Choose KT's Physical Therapy?

Dr. Kushal K. Tiwari (PT), also known as KT Sir, is the Founder of KT's Physical Therapy India — a compassionate and highly experienced Sports Physiotherapist and Hydro Therapist with over 15 years of professional experience in sports injury rehabilitation, musculoskeletal physiotherapy, athletic performance enhancement, and functional recovery.

With an extensive background working alongside National & International Players, Coaches, Trainers, Performance Experts, and Medical Professionals, Dr. Kushal is recognized for developing and implementing effective, evidence-based treatment and rehabilitation programs tailored to the needs of athletes and patients.

Over the years, he has earned recognition for contributing to the successful recovery of more than 15,000 athletes and over 10,000 patients, helping individuals return to sport, improve movement quality, and achieve optimal physical performance.

Dr. Kushal specializes in sports rehabilitation, boxing injury management, hydro therapy, movement assessment, manual therapy, and performance-focused rehabilitation. His athlete-centered approach combines modern rehabilitation science with personalized care to ensure faster recovery, injury prevention, and long-term physical wellness.
