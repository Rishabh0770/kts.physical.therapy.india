How to Book Your Appointment

Easy 7-step process to reserve your therapy session

Quick Overview

Enter Your Information - Name, Phone, Email, Age
Choose a Date - Pick any date from today onwards
Select Time Slot - Pick from available 1-hour slots (10 AM - 6 PM)
Choose Service - Select your treatment type
Describe Condition - Tell us about your pain or injury
Confirm & Pay - Review and proceed to secure payment
Appointment Confirmed! - You'll receive a confirmation
1️⃣
Fill in Your Personal Information
Start by entering your basic details. These help us keep track of your appointments and contact you when needed.
  • Full Name - Your complete name
  • Phone Number - 10-digit mobile number (required for confirmation)
  • Email Address - Optional, but recommended
  • Age - Helps us understand your condition better
Tip: Make sure your phone number is correct - we'll use it to send you confirmation and reminders!
2️⃣
Select Your Preferred Date
Click on the date field to pick the day you want your appointment. You can book from today up to any future date.
  • 📅 Pick any date from today onwards
  • 👶 No restrictions - book anytime that suits you
  • ⏰ Appointment duration: 1 hour per slot
Pro Tip: Book at least 24 hours in advance for better confirmation chances!
3️⃣
Choose Your Time Slot
Once you pick a date, available time slots will appear. Our clinic operates from 10 AM to 6 PM daily.

Real-Time Updates: If someone books a slot while you're browsing, it will immediately show as "Booked" so you won't miss it!
4️⃣
Select Your Treatment
Choose the type of physiotherapy treatment you need from our services.
  • 🏃 Sports Physiotherapy
  • 🏊 Hydrotherapy
  • 🦴 Orthopaedic Rehabilitation
  • 🧠 Neuro Physiotherapy
  • 💉 Dry Needling / IFT
  • 🧘 Postural & Spine Care
  • 📋 General Consultation
  • ❓ Other / Not Sure
Not Sure? Select "General Consultation" or "Other / Not Sure" - our therapist will discuss your condition during the appointment!
5️⃣
Describe Your Condition
Tell us about your pain, injury, or condition. This helps our therapist prepare for your appointment.
  • 💭 Describe where it hurts
  • 📅 How long have you had this issue?
  • 🤕 Any recent injury or incident?
  • 💊 Are you taking any medications?
  • 🏥 Any previous surgeries?
Optional: You don't have to fill this field, but it helps us provide better care!
6️⃣
Review & Confirm
Check all your details are correct, then click "Confirm & Proceed to Payment"
  • ✅ Verify your name and phone number
  • ✅ Check your selected date and time
  • ✅ Confirm the treatment type
  • ✅ Review your condition description
Important: Once you click confirm, your time slot will be reserved!
7️⃣
Complete Payment
You'll be taken to a secure payment page to complete your booking.
  • 💳 Secure payment processing
  • ✅ Multiple payment methods available
  • 📧 Instant confirmation email
  • 📱 WhatsApp confirmation message
All Done! Your appointment is now confirmed. You'll receive a confirmation message shortly!
Frequently Asked Questions
❓ Can I change my appointment after booking?
Yes! Contact us via WhatsApp or phone at least 24 hours before your appointment to reschedule.
❓ What if I need to cancel my appointment?
You can cancel anytime, but cancellations within 24 hours may have a cancellation fee. Contact our clinic directly.
❓ How long is each appointment?
Each appointment is 1 hour long. The time slot you select is for your complete session.
❓ What should I bring to my appointment?
Bring a valid ID, any medical reports related to your condition, and comfortable clothes. Avoid heavy meals 1 hour before.
❓ Do I need to arrive early?
Please arrive 5-10 minutes early for first-time patients to complete any necessary paperwork.
❓ What payment methods do you accept?
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and digital wallets. All payments are secure and encrypted.
❓ What if the slot I want is booked?
Try selecting a different date or time. Slots are released constantly as other patients cancel or reschedule.
❓ Will I get a reminder before my appointment?
Yes! We'll send you a WhatsApp and/or email reminder 24 hours before your appointment.
